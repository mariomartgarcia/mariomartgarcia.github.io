Hello! 👋 I am a Lead Data Scientist at GOVOY, where I work on agentic AI, computer vision and optimization. I hold a PhD in Machine Learning from the [Basque Center for Applied Mathematics](https://www.bcamath.org/es), supervised by [Iñaki Inza](http://www.sc.ehu.es/ccwbayes/members/inaki.htm) and [Jose A. Lozano](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=lhzoWpwAAAAJ&hl=es), where my research focused on learning using privileged information (LUPI): leveraging features that are available during training but not at deployment time. During my PhD, I completed a research stay at [École de Technologie Supérieure (ETS)](https://www.etsmtl.ca/) in Montreal under the supervision of [Marco Pedersoli](https://profs.etsmtl.ca/mpedersoli/). Before that, I obtained my MSc in Data Science from the [Open University of Catalonia](https://www.uoc.edu/es) and my BSc in Physics from the [University of Murcia](https://www.um.es/). I am currently also completing an MBA.

**Interests:** I work in applied AI 🤖, from agentic systems and computer vision to optimization, and keep a research interest in privileged information, knowledge distillation and multi-task learning. I am particularly interested in bringing these techniques into real products and decision-making in clinical and sports environments.
